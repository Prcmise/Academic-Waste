Submission System Under Construction

We are currently developing our online submission platform to provide you with a seamless experience for submitting your research to Academic Waste.

Our new submission system will feature:

  • Easy-to-use manuscript upload interface
  • Real-time submission tracking
  • Integrated peer review management
  • Direct communication with editors

In the meantime, if you would like to submit a manuscript for consideration, please email your submission to submissions@academicwaste.org with the following information:

  • Manuscript title
  • Author names and affiliations
  • Abstract (250 words maximum)
  • Manuscript file (PDF or Word format)
  • Cover letter

Expected launch of online submission system: Spring 2026
