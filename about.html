About the Journal

Academic Waste is now launched and open for submission.

This is a structurally formal, independently operated publication platform designed to archive what currently appears unnecessary in academic production.

We publish research fragments, aesthetically pleasing but epistemically empty data, unfinished hypotheses, and opinions that nobody cares. Some submissions may lack rigor. Some may lack relevance. A few may lack both.

Are they truly useless? Or simply waiting for a different intellectual climate?

Academic Waste does not decide. It archives.

Scope of Publication

We currently accept the following categories:

  • Original Research: Sudden, loosely reasoned, and wildly speculative research that somehow almost sounds convincing — provided that all data are real.
  • Nonsense Data: Results without academic significance but potentially worth sharing.
  • Perspective: Commentary on academic culture, publishing, metrics, institutional behavior, or intellectual trends.
  • Review: Sharp, possibly irreverent overviews of research fields or methodological systems.

Articles are published continuously and are later assembled into issues.

Journal Metrics

  • Annual publication volume: Undetermined.
  • Acceptance rate: Potentially high.
  • Citation count: Likely low.
  • Expected Impact Factor: IF → 0⁺.
  • Average processing time: Variable, depending on the editor-in-chief's mood, energy level, and general existential condition.

Formal Structure

All submissions should include:

  • Title
  • Author(s) (anonymous, pseudonymous, or real names)
  • Abstract
  • Main Text
  • Figures/Tables (if applicable)
  • References (optional)
  • Author Contribution (if ≥2 authors)
  • AI Assistance Disclosure
  • Ethics, Data & Conflict Statement

Academic Waste imitates the structure of a journal. It does not claim institutional authority.

Ethics & Responsibility

We operate under the following baseline principles:

  • No plagiarism
  • No fabrication or falsification
  • All data must be real
  • No copyright violation
  • No illegal acquisition of materials
  • No discriminatory or hateful content

Authors bear full responsibility for their submissions.

Academic Waste is not indexed in academic databases and should not be considered a formal academic credential.

AI Policy

AI tools may be used for language refinement, idea discussion, or illustration drafting. AI systems cannot be listed as authors. Core intellectual responsibility and experimental work must originate from real individuals.

Submission

Submissions are accepted through:

  • Web submission form
  • Official email
  • Direct message channels (as announced)

All accepted articles will be manually archived and assigned an ID.

Academic Waste is an archive of redundancy, latency, and premature dismissal.

If something seems unnecessary today, that is sufficient reason to record it.

We publish what the present is not ready to value.
