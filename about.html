About the Journal

Overview

Academic Waste is an open access journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research, commentary, and analysis across all academic disciplines. We believe in the free and unrestricted dissemination of knowledge to advance scientific understanding and foster intellectual discourse.

Vision

Our vision is to create a platform that challenges conventional academic publishing norms and promotes innovative approaches to scholarly communication. We strive to be a leading voice in the movement toward open science, transparency, and accessibility in research publication.

We envision a future where academic knowledge is freely available to all, where researchers from diverse backgrounds can contribute to the global scientific community, and where the barriers to participation in scholarly discourse are minimized.

Mission

Our mission is to:

  • Promote Open Access: Ensure that all published research is freely available to readers worldwide without subscription barriers or paywalls.
  • Foster Innovation: Encourage novel research approaches and methodologies that push the boundaries of traditional academic inquiry.
  • Maintain Quality: Uphold rigorous peer review standards while streamlining the publication process to reduce time from submission to publication.
  • Encourage Diversity: Welcome contributions from researchers across all disciplines, career stages, and geographical locations.
  • Support Transparency: Promote open peer review, data sharing, and reproducibility in scientific research.
  • Build Community: Create a collaborative environment where researchers can engage in meaningful dialogue and knowledge exchange.

Scope

Academic Waste publishes original research articles, reviews, commentaries, and perspectives across a broad range of academic disciplines including but not limited to:

  • Natural Sciences
  • Social Sciences
  • Humanities
  • Engineering and Technology
  • Medical and Health Sciences
  • Interdisciplinary Studies

Editorial Standards

All submissions undergo rigorous peer review by experts in the relevant field. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of scientific integrity, ethical conduct, and editorial independence. Our editorial process is designed to be fair, efficient, and transparent.
